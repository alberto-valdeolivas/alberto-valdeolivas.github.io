## Structural inspiration

These sites informed broad content and navigation choices. No source code, wording, images, visual branding or layouts were reused.

- [Jure Leskovec](https://cs.stanford.edu/~jure/): concise research positioning and easy access to software.
- [Regina Barzilay](https://www.regina.csail.mit.edu/): organise research around biomedical questions.
- [Michael Schaub](https://michaelschaub.github.io/Publications/): useful publication search and expandable research detail.
- [Charlotte Bunne / AIMM](https://aimm.epfl.ch/): clear research directions and curated highlights.
- [Marinka Zitnik](https://zitniklab.hms.harvard.edu/): lead with scientific purpose and connect different biological scales.

The visual identity builds on the original website's navy and cyan (`#16b1c4`), with a lighter slate-blue background (`#20334d`) and soft blue and periwinkle accents. Layered blue section surfaces keep the page divisions clear. Serif headlines, numbered sections and original vector diagrams preserve the editorial structure of the redesign. All scientific illustrations are generated by `scripts/generate_artwork.rb` and are conceptual, not experimental results.

Typography uses a shared scale in `rem` units: the desktop name and topic labels are 18 px at the default browser size, navigation is 16 px, and reading text is 15–17 px. Supporting labels and captions use 12–14 px. Narrow layouts keep readable text sizes and show the mobile menu at 900 px rather than shrinking the navigation; research topics become a two-column grid.
